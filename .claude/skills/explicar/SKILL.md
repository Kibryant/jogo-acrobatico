---
name: explicar
description: Explains a diff, commit, file or concept to Arthur in Portuguese, at teaching depth. Use it when Arthur asks what something is or why it was done that way, and after any large change.
---

Answer in Portuguese, in this order:

1. **Em uma frase:** what it does.
2. **Por dentro:** how it works, step by step, citing `arquivo:linha`.
3. **Por que assim:** the DEC or F behind it, and what the alternative would have been.
4. **Conceitos:** the Godot, netcode or architecture ideas involved, with a web analogy when it helps.
5. **Como ver funcionando:** the command to run or what to look at in the editor.
6. **O que pode dar errado:** and how Arthur would notice.

Explain every piece of jargon the first time it appears. Short sentences, no filler.
